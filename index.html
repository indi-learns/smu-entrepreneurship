One path, four stages.

Founders need different things at different points. Our programs are organized by where you are, so you can step in at the right place and keep moving.

  1. Stage 1

    Learn

    You're curious about entrepreneurship and want to see how founders think and work.

    • Fireside chats and panels
    • Workshops and seminars
    • Startup treks
    Learn programs
  2. Stage 2

    Engage

    You have an idea, or want real startup experience before you commit to one.

    • Pitch competitions
    • Paid startup internships
    • Mentorship
    Engage programs
  3. Stage 3

    Launch

    You're building. You need structure, mentors, workspace and early funding.

    • Spears Startup LAUNCH
    • Spears LAUNCH Accelerator
    • Student startup funding
    Launch programs
  4. Stage 4

    Scale

    You run a startup with traction and want customers, capital and a network.

    • SMU Cox FinTech Accelerator
    • Southwest Venture Forum
    • Dallas 100
    Scale programs

About the extra feature

Find your stage is a three-question pathfinder. It matches where you are as a founder to one of four stages, recommends the programs to start with, and remembers your result so the programs page and the chatbot can point you to the right place.
