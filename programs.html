Programs for every stage.

The Spears Institute was founded in 2022 with a gift from William S. Spears, the largest by a non-alumnus in SMU history. Its job is to help people start companies. Everything below is grouped by where you are today, from first curiosity to a growing business.

Stage 1

Learn

Your stage

Events and speakers

Open to SMU students; many events open to the community

Fireside chats, panels, seminars, networking sessions and conferences with founders and investors who have built companies.

Stage 3

Launch

Your stage

For student founders who are building a product and need structure, mentors, workspace and early funding.

Spears Startup LAUNCH

SMU student ventures preparing for an accelerator or funding

Dedicated mentors, hands-on workshops and a shared workspace to get your business ready for its next step.

Spears LAUNCH Accelerator

SMU student startups

The institute's signature program: 12 intensive weeks over the summer to turn an idea into a market-ready venture. It ends with Demo Day, where founders pitch to the Dallas startup community.

Student startup funding

SMU student founders

Non-dilutive grants and pre-seed funding to help student ventures get off the ground.

See open applications

Stage 4

Scale

Your stage

For operating startups, on campus or off, that want enterprise customers, investors and a place in the North Texas business community.

SMU Cox FinTech Accelerator

Fintech startups, open to applicants worldwide

Run with MassChallenge, this 16-week program selects up to 15 fintech startups a year and connects them with financial institutions, investors, students and faculty. It is free and takes no equity. It anchors the SMU Cox FinTech Coalition, which also hosts executive forums and an annual FinTech Summit.

Applications for the first cohort close November 19, 2026.

Dallas 100

An annual recognition of the fastest-growing private companies in the Dallas area.
